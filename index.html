💼 Salary Calculation — Moroccan labor & tax law (2026 barème)

Transport & Panier are exempt allowances (within legal limits) and excluded from the taxable base. Frais professionnels = 35% of taxable gross if annual ≤ 78 000 MAD, else 25%, capped at 35 000 MAD/yr. IR uses the 2026 monthly progressive barème (marginal rate − déduction). Dependent relief = 50 MAD/mo each (600/yr), capped at 6, deducted from the IR.

Regular fee formula: fees = Qty × Price × (c_marché + c_interm + c_regl) × (1+VAT) + courier × (1+VAT)
PEA fee formula: fees = [ max(gross × courtage, min) + gross × règlement + gross × bourse ] × (1+TVA)  ·  OPCVM = flat order fee × (1+TVA)  ·  DIV = gross × div-comm × (1+TVA), no TPCVM
BUY → TTC/share = (gross + fees) / qty  ·  SELL → tax = max(proceeds − fees − qty×avgCost, 0) × TPCVM (0 for PEA)
DIV (regular) → tax = gross × (1+VAT) × dividend-rate(year)

🔄 Refresh Prices

Paste your TradingView watchlist block (all columns). The parser handles the multi-line format and MAD/spacing cleanup, updating prices & metrics live.

📅 Import Dividend Calendar

Paste the calendar exactly as copied. Two layouts are auto-detected: Issuer · Amount · Ex-date · Payment date · Type (fields on separate lines or tab-separated), and Issuer · Ex-date · Payment date · Type · Amount with a MAD-suffixed amount last (tab- or space-separated, one row per line). An optional leading Ticker column is also accepted (that's the layout the downloadable template uses). Header rows are auto-skipped, issuers auto-matched to tickers, dates in dd/mm/yyyy.

📁 Transactions — Import / Export CSV

Back up or move your ledger as a .csv. Columns: date,ticker,action,qty,price,pea,opcvm,total,broker (date YYYY-MM-DD; pea=yes/no; opcvm=yes/no; total=TTC; broker=saham/attijari, optional). OPCVM funds export with their total and re-import identically — leave price blank and the unit price is derived from total÷qty (funds are re-classified via the imported OPCVM list). A blank broker auto-resolves (funds→attijari, stocks→saham). Choose Replace to overwrite, or Append to add rows.

🏦 Import OPCVM Prices & Fees

Import the ASFIM/AMMC performance file (.xlsx), keyed by ISIN. There are two variants: the daily file (fresh VL, partial fund coverage) and the fuller weekly file (all funds + buy/sell fees). Held funds are auto-matched by name; map any unmatched fund once and it sticks (via ISIN) for future imports.

🏷️ Import Stock Categories

Categories are entered manually (not from TradingView). A default set is built in — upload an updated .csv only when it changes. Columns: Ticker,Company Name,Category,Economic Cycle,Asset Style. Each row updates that ticker's sector, economic cycle and asset style. Tickers not in the file keep their existing (TradingView) category. Economic cycle & asset style also feed the Rebalance diversification.
